With Bank Rate at 0.10% in 2021, £1 million stuck for three days in a correspondent banking chain cost about £8 in lost interest. At today's 3.75%, the same three days cost about £308. At the 10-year gilt rate, about £453.

From [The Price of Time](/analysis/the-price-of-time/).
