In February 2026, Côte d'Ivoire borrowed for 15 years at an effective 5.39% after hedging into euros. On 1 October, Britain paid 5.51% for 10 years and 6.03% for 30 years in its own currency. At the bottom, the gap remains: 9.5% for DR Congo's debut bond, 10.4% for Kenya's refinancing.
