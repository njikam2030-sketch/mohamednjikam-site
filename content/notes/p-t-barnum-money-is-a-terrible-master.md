---
desk: economics
quote: "Money is a terrible master but an excellent servant."
author: "P. T. Barnum"
order: 19
---
Barnum's line applies to companies as well as individuals. Used well, money finances growth, absorbs shocks and creates options. When the pursuit of short-term financial targets drives every decision, it becomes a master: companies cut investment to meet quarterly numbers, borrow to fund buybacks and lose sight of their customers. A good finance function keeps money in its place: serving the strategy, not replacing it.
