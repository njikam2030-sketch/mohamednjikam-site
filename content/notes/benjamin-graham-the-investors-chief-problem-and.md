---
desk: finance
quote: "The investor’s chief problem, and even his worst enemy, is likely to be himself."
author: "Benjamin Graham, The Intelligent Investor, 1949"
order: 28
---
Graham understood that the greatest danger to returns is behaviour, not information. Fear and greed lead investors to buy high and sell low. Overconfidence leads to concentration and leverage. The best defence is a process: written rules, diversification, regular rebalancing and a refusal to make major decisions in moments of panic or euphoria.
