---
desk: finance
quote: "Bull markets are born on pessimism, grow on skepticism, mature on optimism, and die on euphoria."
author: "Sir John Templeton"
order: 31
---
Templeton's description of the market cycle is a map for investors. At the bottom, everyone is pessimistic and prices are low. At the top, everyone is euphoric and prices are high. Recognising the stage of the cycle is more useful than predicting the next quarter. Signs of euphoria include record issuance, rising leverage and the belief that prices can only go up.
