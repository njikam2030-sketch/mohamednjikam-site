---
desk: leadership
quote: "The important thing is not to stop questioning. Curiosity has its own reason for existing."
author: "Albert Einstein, LIFE magazine, 1955"
order: 54
---
Questioning keeps knowledge alive. Organisations that stop asking questions become rigid; analysts who stop questioning their models become dangerous. Einstein's line is a reminder that certainty is often the enemy of understanding. The best decisions come from teams where questions are welcomed, not punished.
