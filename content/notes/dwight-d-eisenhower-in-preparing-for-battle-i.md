desk: leadership
quote: "In preparing for battle I have always found that plans are useless, but planning is indispensable."
author: "Dwight D. Eisenhower"
order: 43
---
Eisenhower's paradox explains the value of planning. The plan itself rarely survives contact with reality, but the process of planning forces leaders to understand the terrain, the resources and the risks. When events diverge from the plan, a team that has planned together can adapt quickly. Budgets and forecasts in companies serve exactly this function.
