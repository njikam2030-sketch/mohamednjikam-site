Facebook's early motto captured the culture of a generation of technology companies. It produced extraordinary innovation and serious harm: privacy failures, misinformation, damage to institutions that were "broken" along the way. With AI, the stakes are higher. Speed still matters, but the cost of what gets broken means responsible deployment must keep pace.
