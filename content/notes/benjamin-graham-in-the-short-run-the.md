quote: "In the short run, the market is a voting machine but in the long run, it is a weighing machine."
author: "Benjamin Graham, as quoted by Warren Buffett"
order: 27
---
Graham's distinction explains why prices can move far from value. In the short run, prices reflect popularity, fear and momentum. Over the long run, they reflect earnings and cash flows. Patient investors exploit the gap between the two. Impatient ones are its victims. Record IPO volumes in 2026 are, in part, a voting-machine phenomenon: the real test comes when the weighing machine takes over.
