desk: leadership
quote: "It remains Day 1."
author: "Jeff Bezos, letter to Amazon shareholders, 2016"
order: 52
---
Bezos uses "Day 1" to describe the mindset of a start-up: customer obsession, speed and experimentation. "Day 2" is stasis, followed by decline. Large organisations drift towards Day 2 as processes multiply and caution grows. Staying in Day 1 requires deliberate effort: small teams, fast decisions and the willingness to accept mistakes.
