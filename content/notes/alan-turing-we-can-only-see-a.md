quote: "We can only see a short distance ahead, but we can see plenty there that needs to be done."
author: "Alan Turing, 1950"
order: 62
---
Turing's closing sentence is a model of humility about technology. He did not claim to know where machine intelligence would lead, only that there was much work to do. That humility is needed today, when forecasts about artificial intelligence range from utopia to catastrophe. The honest position is to act on what we can see and prepare for what we cannot.
