---
desk: leadership
quote: "Success is a lousy teacher. It seduces smart people into thinking they can’t lose."
author: "Bill Gates, The Road Ahead, 1995"
order: 48
---
Gates's warning applies to individuals, companies and countries. Success breeds overconfidence: the belief that what worked before will keep working. Many dominant companies failed because they could not imagine that their success was temporary. The antidote is to study failures, including near-misses, as carefully as successes.
