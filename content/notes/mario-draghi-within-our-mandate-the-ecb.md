quote: "Within our mandate, the ECB is ready to do whatever it takes to preserve the euro. And believe me, it will be enough."
author: "Mario Draghi, London, 26 July 2012"
order: 34
---
Draghi's three words saved the euro without the ECB spending a single euro at the time. Credibility, not money, did the work: markets believed the central bank would act, so they stopped betting against weaker members. The lesson for policymakers everywhere is that a credible commitment can be more powerful than intervention itself. The lesson for markets is never to fight a central bank that means what it says.
