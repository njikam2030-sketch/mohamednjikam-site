quote: "Data is the new oil."
author: "Clive Humby, 2006"
order: 73
---
Humby's analogy highlights value: raw data, like crude oil, must be refined to become useful. It also suggests concentration: those who control the largest data sets gain advantages that are hard to challenge. For governments, the question is how to ensure data generated by citizens benefits them, and not only the platforms that collect it.
