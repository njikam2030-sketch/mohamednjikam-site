---
desk: leadership
quote: "Stay hungry, stay foolish."
author: "Steve Jobs, Stanford commencement address, 2005"
order: 50
---
Jobs borrowed the phrase from the Whole Earth Catalog. It is a call to keep learning and to keep taking risks, even after success. Hunger keeps ambition alive; foolishness, in this sense, means being willing to try things others consider unrealistic. Combined with discipline, it is the mindset behind most innovations.
