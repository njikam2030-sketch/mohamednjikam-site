---
desk: finance
quote: "Know what you own, and know why you own it."
author: "Peter Lynch"
order: 32
---
Lynch's advice is a defence against fashion. If you cannot explain why you own an asset, you will not know when to sell it, and you will panic when its price falls. For companies, the same applies to every position on the balance sheet: every currency exposure, every investment and every liability should have a reason that someone can explain.
