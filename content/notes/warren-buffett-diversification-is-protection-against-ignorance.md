desk: finance
quote: "Diversification is protection against ignorance."
author: "Warren Buffett"
order: 36
---
Buffett is being deliberately provocative: concentrated positions in businesses you understand deeply can outperform. But for most investors, and for most companies, diversification is the rational response to the limits of knowledge. Nobody can predict which currency, sector or country will suffer the next shock. Spreading exposure is humility applied to a portfolio.
