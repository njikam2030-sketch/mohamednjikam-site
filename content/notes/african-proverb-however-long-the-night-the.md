author: "African proverb"

The proverb speaks of resilience. Economic crises, debt distress and political instability can feel endless. But history shows recoveries, often led by reforms and by the energy of young populations. Resilience is not passive waiting; it is preparing for the dawn by building institutions and skills during the night.
