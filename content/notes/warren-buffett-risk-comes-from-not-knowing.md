Volatility is not the same as risk. The real risk is investing in something you do not understand: a complex structured product, a business model you cannot explain, a market whose rules you do not know. Many of the largest losses in finance came from instruments that even their buyers could not describe. Before any investment or financing decision, I ask: can I explain how this makes or loses money in three sentences?
