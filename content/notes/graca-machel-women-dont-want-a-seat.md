Graça Machel's words go beyond representation. Joining existing institutions on their terms is not enough; the institutions themselves need redesigning. In finance, this means products, governance and access to capital that work for women entrepreneurs, who face persistent barriers to credit across Africa. Redesigning the table is an economic opportunity, not only a question of fairness.
