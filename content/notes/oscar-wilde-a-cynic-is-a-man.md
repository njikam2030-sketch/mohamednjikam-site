---
desk: economics
quote: "A cynic is a man who knows the price of everything and the value of nothing."
author: "Oscar Wilde, Lady Windermere’s Fan, 1892"
order: 16
---
Wilde was writing about people, but the line describes a common failure in finance. Markets are very good at pricing what is traded and measurable, and poor at valuing what is not: trust, institutions, the environment, a company's culture. Good investors know that price and value can diverge for long periods. The opportunity lies in that gap, and so does the risk.
