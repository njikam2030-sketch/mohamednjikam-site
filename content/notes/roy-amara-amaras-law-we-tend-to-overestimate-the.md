quote: "We tend to overestimate the effect of a technology in the short run and underestimate the effect in the long run."
author: "Roy Amara (Amara’s Law)"
order: 66
---
Amara's law describes the typical path of new technologies: inflated expectations, disappointment, then deep and lasting change. The internet followed this path; AI is likely to as well. Investors and companies that understand the pattern avoid both the hype and the cynicism, and position themselves for the long-term transformation.
