Franklin's advice to a young tradesman became my starting point for The Price of Time. For fifteen years of near-zero rates, delay cost almost nothing, and payment systems were built around that illusion. At today's rates, every day money is stuck in a correspondent banking chain is a measurable loss. Time is money again, and the businesses that speed up payments, collections and conversions will earn it back.
