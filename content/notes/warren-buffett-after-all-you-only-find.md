When liquidity is abundant, weak business models and excessive leverage are hidden. When rates rise and credit tightens, they are exposed. That is what is happening in 2026 as the cost of money returns: companies and countries that relied on cheap refinancing are being tested. The time to check whether you are swimming naked is before the tide goes out.
