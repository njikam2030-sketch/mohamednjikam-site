---
desk: leadership
quote: "Management is doing things right; leadership is doing the right things."
author: "Peter Drucker"
order: 39
---
Drucker's distinction is essential for anyone who leads. Management is about efficiency: processes, budgets, execution. Leadership is about direction: choosing what to do and what not to do. A company can be perfectly managed and still fail if it is heading the wrong way. The finance function often focuses on doing things right. Its greatest value comes when it also helps the organisation decide what the right things are.
