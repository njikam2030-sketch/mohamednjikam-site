---
desk: leadership
quote: "Before you are a leader, success is all about growing yourself. When you become a leader, success is all about growing others."
author: "Jack Welch"
order: 46
---
Welch describes a transition many people find difficult. Early in a career, success comes from personal performance. As a leader, it comes from the performance of others. The skills that made someone an excellent analyst do not automatically make them an excellent manager. The shift requires learning to delegate, to coach and to measure success through the team.
