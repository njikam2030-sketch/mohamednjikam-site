---
desk: economics
quote: "Nothing is so permanent as a temporary government programme."
author: "Milton Friedman"
order: 4
---
Every government announces temporary measures in a crisis: subsidies, tax reliefs, emergency spending. Many quietly become permanent because beneficiaries organise to defend them and politicians fear the cost of withdrawal. That is why a temporary bank surcharge that ends after fifteen months, or a fuel duty cut extended "until July", deserves scrutiny. Fiscal credibility depends on whether "temporary" really means temporary. Bond markets have learned to price the difference between the announcement and the likely reality.
