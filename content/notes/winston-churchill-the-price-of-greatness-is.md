desk: leadership
quote: "The price of greatness is responsibility."
author: "Winston Churchill, Harvard, 1943"
order: 44
---
Churchill spoke in 1943, in the middle of a war. The line applies to every position of authority: the higher you rise, the more your decisions affect others, and the less you can blame circumstances. For a CFO, responsibility means signing the accounts, defending forecasts and telling the board uncomfortable truths. Influence without accountability is not leadership.
