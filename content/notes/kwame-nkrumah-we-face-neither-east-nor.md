author: "Kwame Nkrumah, 1960"

Nkrumah's declaration of non-alignment in 1960 remains a powerful idea for Africa today, as the continent navigates competition between the United States, China, Europe, the Gulf and others. Facing forward means choosing partners on the basis of African interests: investment, technology transfer, fair terms of trade. It means refusing to be a pawn in other people's rivalries.
