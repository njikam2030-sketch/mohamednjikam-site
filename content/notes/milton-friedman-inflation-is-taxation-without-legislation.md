---
desk: economics
quote: "Inflation is taxation without legislation."
author: "Milton Friedman"
order: 15
---
Inflation transfers wealth from savers and wage earners to debtors, including governments, without any vote. It reduces the real value of public debt while eroding the purchasing power of fixed incomes. That is why central bank independence matters: it removes the temptation to inflate away debts. It is also why inflation hurts the poor most, since they hold cash and have the least power to renegotiate wages or prices.
