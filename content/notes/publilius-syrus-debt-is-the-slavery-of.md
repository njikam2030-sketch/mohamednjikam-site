---
desk: economics
quote: "Debt is the slavery of the free."
author: "Publilius Syrus, 1st century BC"
order: 17
---
Publilius Syrus wrote two thousand years ago, but the warning fits every era of cheap credit. Debt is a powerful tool: it finances houses, factories and infrastructure. But it also binds future income to past decisions. A country or a company that borrows heavily loses freedom of action exactly when it needs it most, in a crisis. The question is never whether to borrow, but how much flexibility the debt leaves.
