desk: finance
quote: "You never want a serious crisis to go to waste."
author: "Rahm Emanuel, 2008"
order: 37
---
Emanuel's line is often quoted cynically, but it describes something real: crises create the political will for reforms that would be impossible in calm times. Banking regulation after 2008, digital payments after the pandemic, energy policy after supply shocks. The danger is that crises also produce rushed decisions. The best reforms are prepared in advance, so they are ready when the window opens.
