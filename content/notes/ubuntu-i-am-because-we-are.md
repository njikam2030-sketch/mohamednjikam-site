Ubuntu expresses a view of humanity as interconnected. It has practical implications for economics and leadership: trust, community and reciprocity are forms of capital. Informal savings groups, family remittances and community lending across Africa are expressions of this philosophy. Formal finance works best when it builds on these networks rather than ignoring them.
