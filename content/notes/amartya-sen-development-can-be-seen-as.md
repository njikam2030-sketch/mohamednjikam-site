desk: economics
quote: "Development can be seen as a process of expanding the real freedoms that people enjoy."
author: "Amartya Sen, Development as Freedom, 1999"
order: 10
---
Sen changed how we measure progress. GDP growth matters, but development is ultimately about what people are able to do and to be: to be educated, healthy, to participate politically, to choose their work. This perspective is vital for Africa. Growth that enriches a few while leaving schools, clinics and roads behind is not development. The best economic policies expand capabilities: they make it possible for people to use their talent.
