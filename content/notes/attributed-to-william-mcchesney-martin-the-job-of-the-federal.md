The image attributed to William McChesney Martin captures the loneliness of central banking. The right time to tighten is when the economy feels good and nobody wants the party to end. Central bankers who wait until inflation is obvious usually have to tighten much harder later. In 2026, with inflation rising again on energy prices, the Fed and the Bank of England face exactly this dilemma.
