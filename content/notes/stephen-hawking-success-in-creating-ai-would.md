Hawking's full warning continued: it might also be the last, unless we learn to avoid the risks. The statement captures both sides of the debate. It is significant that in 2026 some of the leaders of AI companies themselves have called for slowing the frontier. Serious people disagree about the risks; that disagreement is a reason for caution and oversight.
