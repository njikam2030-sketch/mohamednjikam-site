This is the core idea of my analysis Intelligence on Tap. Every task performed by an AI system is measured and billed in tokens, just as electricity is billed in kilowatt-hours. As AI becomes part of every business process, the token budget will sit alongside payroll and rent. The companies and countries that can afford abundant intelligence will move faster than those that must ration it.
