quote: "Only the paranoid survive."
author: "Andy Grove, title of his 1996 book"
order: 47
---
Grove's title is about vigilance, not fear. Industries change at inflection points that look small at first: a new technology, a new competitor, a change in regulation. Leaders who wait for certainty react too late. A healthy paranoia means constantly asking what could make the current business model obsolete, especially when results are good.
