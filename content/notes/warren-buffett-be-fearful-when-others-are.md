desk: finance
quote: "Be fearful when others are greedy and greedy when others are fearful."
author: "Warren Buffett, letter to Berkshire Hathaway shareholders, 1986"
order: 22
---
This is the simplest investment advice and the hardest to follow. Crowds feel safe; contrarians feel lonely. Yet the best opportunities appear when fear dominates and assets are sold regardless of value, while the greatest risks build up when everyone is confident. In practice, it means keeping cash and discipline during booms so that you can act when others are forced to sell.
