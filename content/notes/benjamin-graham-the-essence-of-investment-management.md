---
desk: finance
quote: "The essence of investment management is the management of risks, not the management of returns."
author: "Benjamin Graham"
order: 29
---
Returns are uncertain; risks can be managed. A disciplined investor decides first how much can be lost, then how much can be gained. This principle applies equally to corporate treasury, where the priority is liquidity and capital preservation, not maximising yield on cash. Many treasury disasters began with the search for a few extra basis points.
