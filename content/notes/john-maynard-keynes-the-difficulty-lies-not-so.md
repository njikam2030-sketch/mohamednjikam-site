---
desk: economics
quote: "The difficulty lies not so much in developing new ideas as in escaping from old ones."
author: "John Maynard Keynes, The General Theory, 1936"
order: 2
---
This is the most honest sentence in economics. Our models, habits and incentives are built around assumptions that once worked. When the world changes, the hardest part is not imagining a new framework but abandoning the comfortable one. The fifteen years of near-zero rates are a perfect example: a whole generation of investors, treasurers and policymakers learned to treat money as free. Unlearning that is painful, and those who resist it pay the bill in higher funding costs and broken business models. Intellectual humility is not modesty; it is a competitive advantage.
