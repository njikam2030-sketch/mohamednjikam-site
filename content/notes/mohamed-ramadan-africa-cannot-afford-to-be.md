From my analysis Intelligence on Tap. If Africa buys artificial intelligence the way it has bought energy, imported and paid for in dollars, it will repeat an old dependency. The alternative is to invest in the foundations: energy, data centres, connectivity and skills, and to build a share of the value chain on the continent.
