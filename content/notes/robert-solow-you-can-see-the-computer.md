quote: "You can see the computer age everywhere but in the productivity statistics."
author: "Robert Solow, New York Times Book Review, 1987"
order: 14
---
Solow wrote this in 1987. The productivity gains from computers did eventually appear, in the late 1990s, after companies reorganised around the technology. The same question now hangs over artificial intelligence. Investment is enormous; measurable productivity gains are still modest. History suggests the gains come, but only when organisations redesign how work is done, not when they simply add new tools to old processes. The lag matters for investors who are financing the build-out today.
