quote: "Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall."
author: "Proverbs 16:18"
order: 56
---
The proverb describes a pattern repeated throughout financial history: the bank that believed it was too strong to fail, the investor convinced of his own genius, the government certain that markets would always lend. Pride blinds decision-makers to risks they would otherwise see. Humility is not weakness; it is protection.
