quote: "If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together."
author: "African proverb"
order: 85
---
The proverb describes a trade-off every leader faces. Acting alone is quicker; building coalitions is slower but lasts longer. Regional integration, partnerships and team-building all require patience. For Africa's economic development, the long road together, through regional markets and shared infrastructure, is the one that leads furthest.
