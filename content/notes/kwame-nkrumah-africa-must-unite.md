Nkrumah's call for unity is finding a modern, economic form in the African Continental Free Trade Area. Fragmented markets limit scale, investment and industrialisation. A continental market of over a billion people changes the calculation for investors. The challenge is implementation: removing non-tariff barriers, connecting infrastructure and integrating payment systems.
