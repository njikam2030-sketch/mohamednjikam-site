---
desk: africa
quote: "The most potent weapon in the hands of the oppressor is the mind of the oppressed."
author: "Steve Biko, 1971"
order: 93
---
Biko understood that oppression works through minds as well as laws. Confidence, self-belief and a sense of agency are preconditions for change. In economic terms, it is a reminder that development requires belief in one's own capacity to build institutions, companies and markets, not only external support.
