Skinner's line is more relevant now than when he wrote it. The danger of AI in organisations is not that machines will think, but that people will stop thinking: accepting outputs without verification, delegating judgement along with tasks. The value of human professionals will lie in exactly the critical thinking that automation can tempt us to abandon.
