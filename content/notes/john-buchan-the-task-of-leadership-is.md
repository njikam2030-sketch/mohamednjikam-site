---
desk: leadership
quote: "The task of leadership is not to put greatness into people, but to elicit it, for the greatness is there already."
author: "John Buchan"
order: 42
---
Buchan describes leadership as discovery rather than command. Most people have more capacity than their roles allow them to use. Good leaders create conditions in which that capacity emerges: clear goals, trust, responsibility and recognition. It is also a reminder that the best teams are built, not bought.
