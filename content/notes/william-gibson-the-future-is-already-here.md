Gibson's observation is about inequality. New technologies arrive first in a few places, for a few people. The distribution of AI capabilities, between countries, companies and individuals, will shape economic power for decades. For Africa, the challenge is to make sure the future arrives broadly and on fair terms, not as an expensive import.
