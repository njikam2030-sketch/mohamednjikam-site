desk: africa
quote: "He who feeds you, controls you."
author: "Thomas Sankara"
order: 92
---
Sankara's warning is about dependence. Countries that rely on imports or aid for basic needs lose policy autonomy. The same logic applies to finance: dependence on foreign currency borrowing exposes countries to decisions made elsewhere. Building domestic capacity, in food, energy and capital, is a form of sovereignty.
