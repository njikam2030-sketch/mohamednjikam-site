Bezos's warning to incumbents is a law of competition: high margins attract competitors. Businesses that rely on opaque pricing or customer inertia are vulnerable to anyone who offers more for less. Cross-border payments are a clear example: high fees and slow transfers have created space for new providers. In the long run, excess margins are temporary unless they are protected by real advantages.
