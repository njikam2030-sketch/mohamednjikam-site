Krugman's line explains why some countries grow rich and others stall. Over decades, living standards depend on how much each hour of work produces. Britain's productivity slowdown since 2008 is, in my view, the single most important economic story behind its fiscal difficulties: without productivity growth, every promise on public services must be paid for with higher taxes or more debt. For Africa, the same principle explains why investment in infrastructure, energy and skills matters more than any short-term stimulus.
