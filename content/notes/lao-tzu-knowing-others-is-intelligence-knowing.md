---
desk: leadership
quote: "Knowing others is intelligence; knowing yourself is true wisdom."
author: "Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching"
order: 55
---
Lao Tzu describes the difference between analysis and wisdom. Understanding markets, competitors and colleagues is valuable. Understanding your own biases, limits and emotions is more valuable still, because it determines how you use everything else you know. Self-knowledge is the foundation of humility, and humility is the foundation of good judgement.
