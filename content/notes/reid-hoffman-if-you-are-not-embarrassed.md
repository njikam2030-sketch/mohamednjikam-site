desk: leadership
quote: "If you are not embarrassed by the first version of your product, you’ve launched too late."
author: "Reid Hoffman"
order: 51
---
Hoffman's advice is about speed of learning. Waiting for perfection delays feedback from real users. A modest first version, launched early, teaches more than months of internal debate. The principle applies beyond technology: new financial products, internal processes and strategies benefit from early testing.
