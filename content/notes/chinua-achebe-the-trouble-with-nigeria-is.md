quote: "The trouble with Nigeria is simply and squarely a failure of leadership."
author: "Chinua Achebe, The Trouble with Nigeria, 1983"
order: 80
---
Achebe's diagnosis applies far beyond Nigeria. Natural resources, population and talent are not enough without leaders who build institutions, fight corruption and put the public interest first. The good news in his argument is that leadership can change. Countries that improved their governance have improved their economic trajectory.
