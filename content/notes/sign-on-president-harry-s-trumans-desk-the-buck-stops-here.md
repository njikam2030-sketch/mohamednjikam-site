Truman's desk sign is the clearest statement of accountability in public life. Decisions can be delegated; responsibility cannot. Organisations become dysfunctional when everyone can approve and nobody is accountable. Clear ownership of each decision, with a named person responsible for the outcome, is one of the simplest and most powerful improvements any organisation can make.
