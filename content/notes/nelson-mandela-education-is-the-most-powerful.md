quote: "Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world."
author: "Nelson Mandela, 2003"
order: 57
---
Mandela understood that lasting change comes from people's capabilities. Education raises productivity, strengthens institutions and gives individuals the tools to shape their own lives. For Africa, investment in education is investment in the most productive asset there is. It is also why YouthShare Initiatives and similar efforts matter: they invest in the next generation.
