---
desk: economics
quote: "Inflation is always and everywhere a monetary phenomenon."
author: "Milton Friedman, 1970"
order: 3
---
Friedman's line is a starting point, not the whole story. Over long periods, sustained inflation does require too much money chasing too few goods. But 2022 and the energy shocks since have shown that supply disruptions, wars and energy prices can drive inflation in the short run, and that central banks then face a choice: accommodate the shock or fight its spread into wages and expectations. The enduring truth in Friedman's sentence is accountability. Whatever the trigger, it is monetary policy that decides whether a price shock becomes a permanent inflation problem.
