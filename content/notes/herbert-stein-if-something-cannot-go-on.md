quote: "If something cannot go on forever, it will stop."
author: "Herbert Stein, economist (Stein’s Law)"
order: 11
---
Stein's Law sounds obvious, which is why it is so often ignored. Current account deficits, rising debt ratios, asset prices growing faster than incomes, companies borrowing to pay dividends: all can continue for a surprisingly long time, and then stop abruptly. The value of the law is not in predicting when, but in forcing us to identify which trends are unsustainable and to prepare for their end before the market forces the adjustment.
