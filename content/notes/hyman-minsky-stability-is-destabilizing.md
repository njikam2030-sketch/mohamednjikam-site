quote: "Stability is destabilizing."
author: "Hyman Minsky"

---
Minsky's insight is that long periods of calm encourage risk-taking. When nothing has gone wrong for years, lenders relax standards, borrowers take more leverage and investors treat volatility as a thing of the past. The system becomes fragile precisely because it looks safe. The 2008 crisis was a Minsky moment, and so, on a smaller scale, are many sudden reversals in emerging-market debt. Risk management means being most cautious when everyone else feels most comfortable.
