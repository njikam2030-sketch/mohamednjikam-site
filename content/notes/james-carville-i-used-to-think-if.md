author: "James Carville, 1993"

Carville's joke contains a serious truth: bond markets can discipline governments more effectively than voters. In 1993, it was the Clinton administration. In 2022, it was Britain's mini-budget. In 2026, gilt yields above 6% shaped the Chancellor's choices before a single vote was cast. For African finance ministers, this intimidation has been familiar for decades.
