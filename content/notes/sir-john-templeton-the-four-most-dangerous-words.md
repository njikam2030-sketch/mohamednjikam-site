quote: "The four most dangerous words in investing are: this time it’s different."
author: "Sir John Templeton"
order: 30
---
Every bubble has its story about why the old rules no longer apply: the internet, financial innovation, emerging markets, crypto, artificial intelligence. Sometimes the technology really is transformative. But valuations, leverage and human behaviour rarely change. The new era usually turns out to be the old cycle in new clothes.
