Buffett's distinction is the foundation of investing. A good company bought at an excessive price can be a poor investment; an ordinary company bought cheaply can be an excellent one. In a market dominated by a few large technology names, the distinction matters more than ever. Enthusiasm for a theme, such as artificial intelligence, can push prices well above any reasonable estimate of future cash flows. Value is earned slowly; price can change overnight.
