Everyone will use AI, just as everyone uses electricity. The decisive questions are who produces it, who finances it and who sets its price. Those who own the infrastructure, the chips, the data centres and the energy, will hold power comparable to the oil majors of the last century. That is why AI is a question of industrial and financial strategy, not just technology.
