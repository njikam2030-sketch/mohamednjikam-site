author: "African proverb, popularised by Chinua Achebe"

This proverb is about narrative power. For centuries, Africa's story was told by others. The same is true in finance: African risk is often assessed by institutions far from the continent, sometimes with outdated assumptions. African voices in economic analysis, ratings and media matter because they change how capital is priced.
