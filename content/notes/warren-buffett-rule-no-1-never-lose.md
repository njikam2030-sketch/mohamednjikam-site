Buffett's rule is a statement about the mathematics of losses. A 50% loss requires a 100% gain to recover. Avoiding large losses matters more than capturing every gain. In practice, it means understanding what you own, refusing leverage that can force a sale at the worst time, and treating the downside as seriously as the upside. Preservation of capital is the first job of any treasurer.
