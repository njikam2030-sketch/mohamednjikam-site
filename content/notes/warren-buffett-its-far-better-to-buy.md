---
desk: finance
quote: "It’s far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price."
author: "Warren Buffett, letter to Berkshire Hathaway shareholders, 1989"
order: 23
---
Buffett learned this from Charlie Munger, after years of buying mediocre businesses cheaply. Quality compounds: a company with durable competitive advantages and good management keeps creating value long after the purchase. A cheap, poor business often stays cheap or gets cheaper. For investors in emerging markets, the lesson is to focus on governance and durability, not just low valuation multiples.
