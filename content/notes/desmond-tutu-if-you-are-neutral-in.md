---
desk: leadership
quote: "If you are neutral in situations of injustice, you have chosen the side of the oppressor."
author: "Desmond Tutu"
order: 60
---
Tutu's statement challenges the comfort of neutrality. Silence in the face of wrongdoing favours those with power. In organisations, it means that leaders who ignore misconduct or unfair treatment are not neutral; they are complicit. Integrity sometimes requires taking a position, even at personal cost.
