---
desk: finance
quote: "But how do we know when irrational exuberance has unduly escalated asset values?"
author: "Alan Greenspan, Washington, 5 December 1996"
order: 33
---
Greenspan asked the question in 1996, three years before the dot-com peak. Markets rose for years after his warning, which shows how hard it is to time a bubble. The question remains relevant in 2026, as valuations of AI-related companies and record IPO volumes raise the same concerns. The honest answer is that we usually know only afterwards, which is why prudence matters more than prediction.
