---
desk: leadership
quote: "The most important thing in communication is hearing what isn’t said."
author: "Peter Drucker"
order: 40
---
In organisations, the most important information is often missing from the report: the risk nobody mentions, the doubt nobody voices, the customer who quietly leaves. Leaders who only read what is presented to them decide on a cleaner story than reality. Listening for silence means asking direct questions, visiting the front line and making it safe for people to bring bad news.
