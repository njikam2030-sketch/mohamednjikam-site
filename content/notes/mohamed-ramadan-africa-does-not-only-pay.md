From The Price of Time. African economies pay higher interest on debt and higher fees on remittances. Sending $200 to sub-Saharan Africa costs 8.46% on average, against 6.36% worldwide. In a world of higher rates, slow and expensive payments are a hidden tax. Reducing that cost is one of the most direct ways to keep more value on the continent.
