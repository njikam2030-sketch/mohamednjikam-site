quote: "Seek ye first the political kingdom and all else shall be added unto you."
author: "Kwame Nkrumah"
order: 78
---
Nkrumah believed political independence was the precondition for economic progress. History has shown that independence was necessary but not sufficient: institutions, governance and economic policy determined what each country made of its freedom. The economic kingdom has to be built deliberately, through investment, education and sound management of public finances.
