desk: leadership
quote: "Leadership is the capacity to translate vision into reality."
author: "Warren Bennis"
order: 41
---
A vision without execution is a slogan. Bennis puts the emphasis on translation: turning ambition into priorities, budgets, people and timelines. Many strategies fail not because they are wrong but because nobody turns them into concrete decisions. Finance plays a central role here, by allocating capital to the priorities the strategy names and refusing to fund those it does not.
