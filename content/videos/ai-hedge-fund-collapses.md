Bloomberg Businessweek on a hedge fund built around the AI trade that came undone when earnings disappointed at the largest technology companies. It is a compact illustration of concentration risk: when a handful of companies drive both the market and the strategy, a single bad quarter can travel a long way. The same logic applies to the debt now financing the AI build-out, as I argue in Borrowing for Intelligence.
