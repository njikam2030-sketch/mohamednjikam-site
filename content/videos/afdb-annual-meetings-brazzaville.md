The African Development Bank closed its 2026 Annual Meetings in Brazzaville, in the Republic of Congo. These gatherings rarely make headlines outside the continent, which is a pity: they are where finance ministers, central bankers and development lenders argue about the questions that decide Africa's cost of capital, from debt relief to local-currency lending. Worth watching for anyone who follows the continent's financing needs, the theme of my essay The Arithmetic of Impatience.
