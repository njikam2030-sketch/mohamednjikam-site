Bloomberg Surveillance on the day of the Federal Reserve's 16 September decision: a quarter-point rise to 3.75-4 per cent, its first increase since 2023, with most officials expecting to go further. The programme captures how quickly market expectations shifted. Washington moved first; London is expected to follow on 5 November.
