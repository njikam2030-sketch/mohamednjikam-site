Standard Chartered's wealth team sets out its view of hedge fund strategies for 2026 and the role they can play in a diversified portfolio. Treat it as what it is, a manager's outlook rather than independent research. Its interest lies in the reasoning: in a year when equities and bonds have at times fallen together, investors are looking harder for returns that do not depend on either.
