PIMCO argues that wider credit spreads on some AI borrowers are a sign of healthy market discipline rather than a warning. I agree, with a caveat. Discipline at the margin is welcome; the risk would be spreads staying tight for the largest names because investors assume they can never disappoint. That distinction is at the heart of Borrowing for Intelligence.
